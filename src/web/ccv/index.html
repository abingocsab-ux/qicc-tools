Module 04

Troubleshooting Guide

Immediate corrective actions for defects during CV line operation.

⚠ SAFETY FIRST
Operator and equipment safety always come before production output. For any defect marked Critical, stop the line, follow site lock-out / evacuation procedure if instructed, and contact Maintenance immediately.
General Escalation Protocol
  1. Stop or slow the line as indicated by the defect's severity rating.
  2. Inform your Supervisor and the Process Engineer of the observed defect.
  3. Contact Maintenance directly for any mechanical, electrical, pressure, or sealing issue.
  4. Do not attempt unauthorized adjustments to pressurized systems, heaters, or electrical components.
  5. Record the defect, action taken, and outcome in the shift log for traceability.
